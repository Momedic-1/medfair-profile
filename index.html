Who we are

Mission

Give people fast access to licensed doctors, then help them finish care through trusted facilities when a consult alone is not enough.

Too many people wait too long, travel too far, or pay too much for basic medical advice. MedFair exists to shorten that gap — for rural communities, organisation members, and anyone who needs a doctor sooner.

How MedFair works

Digital access. Physical completion.

We start care on a screen and finish it where it is needed — consult, diagnostics, medicines, and follow-up through one accountable pathway.

01 Consult

Members book and join a licensed GP or specialist by video, with clinical notes kept on the platform.

02 Refer

When tests or medicines are needed, MedFair authorises a visit to a partner hospital or facility.

03 Complete

Review, laboratory work, imaging, and pharmacy fulfilment close the episode of care.

What guides us

Core values

Five commitments we hold when we build, partner, and deliver care.

Access first

Care should reach people where they are, not only where hospitals are concentrated.

Clinical responsibility

Licensed doctors, proper notes, and no shortcuts on quality.

Complete care

A consult is not enough if the patient still needs tests or medicines. Finish the episode.

Trust and clarity

Clear pricing, clear authorisation, and clear records for patients and partners.

Partnership

Hospitals, HMOs, organisations, and clinicians are part of delivery — not afterthoughts.
